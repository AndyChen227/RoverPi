## English

### Channel 1 motor test and DualSense input validation

### Verified today

- Confirmed that motor-driver Channel 1 controls the two left-side motors.
- Tested Channel 1 with `gpiozero` using BCM GPIO12 (`PWM1`), GPIO23 (`INA1`), and GPIO24 (`INB1`).
- After a 3-second warning delay, both left-side motors moved successfully at 30% PWM for about 1 second and then stopped.
- The observed direction appeared forward, but this remains provisional until full-rover orientation and polarity are validated.
- Paired, connected, and trusted a PS5 DualSense Wireless Controller over Bluetooth.
- During this session Linux exposed the main controller as `js0` and `/dev/input/event11`, motion sensors as `js1` and `/dev/input/event12`, and the touchpad as `/dev/input/event13`.
- Initially recorded the `jstest` Y-axis signs incorrectly. Re-validation on August 10 established the correct mapping: forward push `-32767`, center `0`, backward pull `+32767`. Python `evdev` uses a different observed scale; see the August 10 log.
- Installed/used the `python3-evdev` and joystick packages to validate the controller from Linux and Python.

### Important limits

- `/dev/input/event*` numbers can change after reconnecting or rebooting; discover the current device instead of treating `event11` as permanent.
- Right-side motor control has not been verified.
- Full-rover forward, backward, turning, and gamepad-controlled driving have not been implemented or verified.

### Next steps

- Verify Channel 2 and the right-side motors with the same controlled test pattern.
- Confirm actual rover-relative motor directions with the chassis safely supported.
- Replace the fixed gamepad event path with robust device discovery.
- Map controller input to motor commands only after both motor channels and stop behavior are verified.

### Channel 1 电机测试与 DualSense 输入验证

### 今日已验证

- 确认电机驱动板 Channel 1 控制左侧两个电机。
- 使用 `gpiozero` 测试 Channel 1：BCM GPIO12（`PWM1`）、GPIO23（`INA1`）和 GPIO24（`INB1`）。
- 等待 3 秒安全提示后，左侧两个电机以 30% PWM 成功转动约 1 秒，随后停止。
- 观察到的方向看起来是前进，但在整车方向和极性验证完成前，这仍是暂定判断。
- 通过蓝牙完成 PS5 DualSense 无线手柄的配对、连接和信任设置。
- 本次会话中，Linux 将主控制器识别为 `js0` 和 `/dev/input/event11`，运动传感器识别为 `js1` 和 `/dev/input/event12`，触摸板识别为 `/dev/input/event13`。
- 当天最初把 `jstest` 的 Y 轴正负方向记反了。8 月 10 日重新验证后的正确结果是：前推 `-32767`、居中 `0`、后拉 `+32767`；Python `evdev` 使用另一套数值范围，详见 8 月 10 日日志。
- 安装并使用 `python3-evdev` 和 joystick 软件包，通过 Linux 与 Python 完成手柄验证。

### 重要边界

- 重新连接或重启后，`/dev/input/event*` 编号可能变化；不应将 `event11` 当作永久路径。
- 右侧电机控制尚未验证。
- 整车前进、后退、转向以及手柄控制行驶尚未实现或验证。

### 下一步

- 使用相同的受控测试方式验证 Channel 2 和右侧电机。
- 在安全架空底盘的情况下确认相对于整车的实际电机方向。
- 将固定的手柄 event 路径改为可靠的设备自动发现。
- 只有在两个电机通道和停止行为均验证后，才把手柄输入映射到电机命令。
